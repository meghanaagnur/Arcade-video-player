Midnight Arcade

Every video is a stage. Watch to score, finish to clear it, and put your initials on the cabinet's high-score table.

How to play

Drag the coin into the slot to start (or click it)

  • Space Play / pause
  • ←→ Skip 10s
  • ↑↓ Volume
  • M Sound
  • C Subtitles
  • F Big mode
  • 100 points a second, times your combo (up to x4)
  • Pause or skip and the combo breaks
  • Reach the end without skipping: +2,000
  • Continue replays and keeps your score
